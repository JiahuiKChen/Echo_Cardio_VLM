## Study design and estimand

We will conduct a retrospective revalidation using a deterministic, one-study-per-subject cohort from MIMIC-IV-ECHO. The scientific question is whether frozen echocardiographic representations add predictive information to available structured measurements for completion of observed quantitative report labels. The outcome is agreement with recorded labels. It is not direct image measurement, physiological ground truth, or accuracy for labels that were never reported.

The selected cohort contains 4,530 studies from 4,530 subjects. The SCC input audit verified the original subject-study ownership and split: 3,171 training, 679 validation, and 680 test subjects. C3 reconstructed 184,570 clip vectors and 4,525 study vectors. The exact five prespecified no-cine studies were excluded from every prepared primary modality, yielding imaging-eligible counts of 3,168/678/679. A separately labeled structured-only full-availability sensitivity may retain them, with its own denominator and no paired incremental-value claim.

Historical test results were previously inspected during development of the accepted abstract. We therefore describe this work as prespecified revalidation on a historical split, not untouched independent validation. We retain the existing split rather than create an apparently fresh split after historical exposure. The lock, subsequent changes, and prior exposure will be disclosed.

## Imaging representation

The completed C3 pipeline used a frozen EchoPrime video encoder and deterministic study mean pooling. It did not use the complete native EchoPrime retrieval system. Checkpoint, environment, source-object, clip-membership, embedding-index, and final-output authorities are bound by the preserved production artifacts.

DICOM pixels were decoded through the stored-color raw API, with explicit color provenance and canonical RGB conversion. Monochrome channels were replicated; supported YBR pixels were converted once. An occupancy/motion-derived sector mask was applied before spatial and temporal preprocessing. The ordinary spatial path takes a central square crop, removes 10% from each edge, and resizes to 224 × 224 with cubic interpolation. Long cines use 32 endpoint-inclusive, integer-spaced frames; short cines retain source frames and repeat the final frame. Bounded pixel-driven spatial/temporal fallback paths retain nondegenerate signal and record the chosen path. The encoder uses alternating samples, yielding 16 frames, with the frozen EchoPrime channel normalization. It produces a 512-dimensional vector per eligible clip. Study vectors are means in stable clip-key order, accumulated in float64 and stored as float32.

The production path does not establish the absence of in-sector numerical overlays, calipers, tracing contours, or acquisition-mode cues. It also does not certify a B-mode-only or A4C-only collection. We will report those properties only after a blinded inspection of the actual postprocessed inputs. The [input-content audit](input_content_audit_and_sensitivity.md) specifies this distinction and records the retired-cache constraint.

## Model fitting and validation

Continuous outcomes use Ridge regression across all three modalities, with alpha values 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100, and 1000; intercept enabled; `lsqr`, tolerance `1e-8`, and maximum 10,000 iterations. Selection minimizes validation MAE, with an exact tie choosing the larger alpha. The primary model is fit on training only, without a training-plus-validation refit.

Allowed structured features require at least `max(20, ceil(0.05 × n_train))` finite training observations and at least two distinct values. Median imputation and continuous-feature scaling are learned only on the target-specific training rows. Vision dimensions are scaled using training statistics. The primary structured/fusion models include unscaled missingness indicators only for eligible features with both observed and missing training values. A mandatory sensitivity repeats them without indicators on the same rows.

Primary binary LVEF is strictly `<40%` and uses separately fitted, class-weighted L2 logistic regression. The common C grid is 0.001 through 1000 by factors of ten, solver `liblinear`, tolerance `1e-4`, maximum 5,000 iterations, and seed 20260801. Validation AUROC selects C; an exact tie chooses the smaller C. A validation-only Platt calibrator and Youden operating point are frozen before test evaluation. Their reuse of the same validation set after hyperparameter selection is disclosed as a limitation; calibration is secondary. Ridge-derived threshold classification is a secondary coherence analysis. `<=40%` and `<50%` are prespecified sensitivities and cannot replace the historical `<40%` primary based on results.

## Outcomes and paired inference

Continuous LVEF MAE in EF percentage points is the primary anchor. Secondary metrics include RMSE, signed error, R², correlation, calibration, and tolerance coverage. Each panel task retains its native-unit MAE, uncertainty, and denominator. The strict-panel primary summary is the unweighted mean of each locked task's MAE divided by its training-target IQR. No favorable task subset replaces the full panel.

We will estimate fusion minus vision and fusion minus structured contrasts; structured minus vision is secondary. With lower error favorable, negative error contrasts favor fusion. Binary AUROC contrasts have the opposite favorable direction. We will use 10,000 paired subject bootstrap draws, seed 20260801, with fixed fitted models, preprocessing, calibration, and operating points. One subject-multiplicity vector is shared across modalities and, for the panel, all tasks. A replicate with any undefined locked task is invalid for the panel macro. We will report valid draws, undefined draws, and percentile 95% intervals.

The null-centered two-sided bootstrap p-value is `(1 + count(|d* − d| ≥ |d|)) / (B_valid + 1)`, capped at one. The core Holm family contains exactly four claims: the two fusion contrasts for continuous LVEF MAE and the two for the locked strict-panel mean normalized MAE. This family cannot activate before the strict panel is locked. The two primary-binary AUROC fusion contrasts use a separate secondary Holm family. Intervals condition on the selected fitted models and omit model-training variability.

The LVEF paired-MAE margin of 1 EF point, sensitivities of 0.5 and 2 points, tolerance bands, and error-coverage thresholds are research choices classified as expert inference. They are not established MCIDs, clinical equivalence, or noninferiority margins. Other tasks receive margin-based classifications only after an independent endpoint-specific margin is locked; native-unit errors and paired intervals remain reportable without such a margin.
