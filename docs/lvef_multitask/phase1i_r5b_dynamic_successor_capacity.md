# Phase 1I-R5B-R1 dynamic successor-capacity authority

## Scope and diagnosis

The frozen Phase 1E-F receipt remains an exact historical allocation record.
Its parser and validator still require the original research and backed quota
and file-quota values.  The defect was that the fresh-full-successor route also
sent a current observation through that historical equality gate, so a valid
administrative research-quota increase was rejected before the already-dynamic
successor arithmetic could govern.  This confirms the R5B-R1 static-capacity
incompatibility without reinterpreting any historical receipt.

The repair is additive.  It introduces a closed current-capacity observation
and restricted source receipt in
`capture_lvef_c3_post_reallocation_capacity.py`, then binds that exact receipt
into a version-2 fresh successor-capacity authority and version-2 fresh claim
in `lvef_c3_full_sequential.py`.  Historical receipt schemas, historical claim
schema version 1, scientific stages, source selection, scheduler topology, and
the fixed successor demand remain unchanged.

## Dynamic authority

One dynamic capture records the exact native-quota, pquota, findmnt, df, path,
and mount control evidence and derives:

- `ALLOCATION_NOT_YET_VISIBLE`;
- `BLOCKED_ADDITIONAL_STORAGE_REQUIRED`; or
- `PASS_FRESH_SUCCESSOR_WITH_200GB_RESERVE`.

The validator independently closes the command roles, mount reconciliation,
receipt bytes and hashes, cache counts, successor-root/claim absence, and all
quota, physical-space, and file-slot arithmetic.  Research allocation growth
is data, not a source constant; research-allocation regression and an altered
backed tier fail with distinct authority codes.

The owner-private seal is one no-clobber pair under the existing fixed
`owner_private` directory: a restricted receipt and an aggregate-safe summary.
Both leaves must be regular owner-0600, single-link, no-follow files.  The pair
reader holds both leaf descriptors and one bound parent descriptor throughout
double reads and final full-identity checks, rejecting hardlinks, FIFOs,
symlinks, leaf replacement, parent replacement, and same-size in-place rewrite
races.  The aggregate-safe policy profile is validated before the sole live
probe.

## Full-successor route

Production seal mode is the only route that runs the dynamic command set.  It
reuses that same validated capture for conditional integrated no-body
preflight.  Ordinary production preflight and future claim creation reopen the
fixed sealed pair and do not repeat the primary native-quota observation.  A
missing, stale, non-PASS, noncanonical, mutated, synthetic-authority, or
collision-bearing pair fails before creating an attempt root or claim.

The fresh successor-capacity version-2 artifact binds the canonical dynamic
observation hash and exact restricted-receipt byte count and SHA-256.  A fresh
version-2 claim binds that receipt SHA-256, and the attempt retains an exact
byte copy for adoption.  Frozen R3E/R4D2C replay continues to validate the
unchanged historical version-1 claim contract.
