## 1. Executive clinical interpretation

**Clearest report-completion rationale.** The strongest candidates are measurements that are (a) directly traced or sampled from images a whole-study video encoder actually sees, (b) defined by a single society-standardized acquisition, and (c) individually actionable. These are `lvef`, `left*ventricular*end*diastolic*diameter`, `left*ventricular*end*systolic*diameter`, `septal*thickness`, `inf*lat*thickness`, `la*dimen`, `la*4ch*length`, `ra*length`, `rv*diam`, `tricuspid*annular*plane*systolic*excursion`, `ivc*diam`, `av*pk*vel`, `lvot*vti`, `lvot*diam`, `mv*peak*e`, `mv*peak*a`, `sept*e*prime`, `lat*e*prime`, `tricuspid*regurgitant*peak*velocity`, `sinus*diam`, and `ascending*aorta_diameter`. Each maps to an ASE/EACVI-defined view, phase, and unit, and most carry a guideline-anchored abnormality cutoff.[1][2][3][4]

**Mainly formula or metadata reconstruction.** `fs` is algebraically determined by LVIDd and LVIDs (FS = (LVIDd − LVIDs)/LVIDd), and its clinical standing is weak — it rests on a single linear chord and fails with septal flattening or regional asynergy. `tr[5][6]*mmhg` is the simplified Bernoulli transform of TR velocity (4v²) and adds no independent image information; ESC/ERS explicitly recommend using peak TRV rather than derived pressures as the assignment variable, because derived variables amplify measurement error. Both should be treated as formula-only controls, scored for internal consistency rather than as clinical endpoints. `body*[7][8][9]surface*area` is a closed-form function of height and weight and is metadata reconstruction, not measurement. `height*[10][11]cm` is patient metadata with no echocardiographic acquisition. `resting*sbp`, `resting*dbp`, and `resting_hr` are cuff/monitor values transcribed into the report; they are hemodynamic context that materially conditions interpretation of AS gradients, diastolic indices, and LVEF loading, but predicting them from video is closer to an artificial benchmark than to report completion.[12][13] Recommended disposition: context-only, and reported separately from echo-measurement metrics.

**Definitions or units that block interpretation.** Several targets cannot be adjudicated without restricted raw metadata:

- `mitral_e_velocity` versus `mv_peak_e`. Both plausibly denote peak transmitral E velocity, but one may be an annular or alternate-cycle field, or a differently-derived duplicate. Merging is a reasonable hypothesis but must not be executed before raw alias/unit inspection — silently merging two different fields would corrupt both the reference distribution and any margin.
- `arch_diam`. The aortic arch is not a single anatomic level; ACC/AHA require that measurement level and technique be reported explicitly, and echocardiographic arch imaging (suprasternal) is far less standardized than root or ascending measurements. Without the level and edge convention, cross-study error comparison is not interpretable.[14][15]
- `sinus_diam` and `ascending_aorta_diameter`. Interpretable only with the edge convention stated: echocardiography conventionally uses leading-edge to leading-edge perpendicular to flow, whereas CT/MR uses inner-to-inner sinus-to-sinus. A few millimetres of systematic offset is convention-dependent, not model error.[15]
- `la_dimen`. Ambiguous between M-mode/2D anteroposterior parasternal dimension and other linear conventions; ASE regards linear LA dimension as an inadequate surrogate for LA size and recommends indexed volume, so this is a scorable report field with weak standalone clinical meaning.[16][17]
- `ivc_diam`. Requires respiratory phase, sniff/collapse status, and view; IVC-based RAP assumptions have been shown invasively inaccurate.[8]
- Units. With repository units marked UNKNOWN, cm-versus-mm and m/s-versus-cm/s ambiguities affect `lvef` (% vs fraction), all linear dimensions, all velocities, and `tr_mmhg`. No native-unit tolerance can be finalized until units are verified.

**Localization limitation.** For every dimensional and Doppler target, a clinically deployable tool would require caliper/trace localization on the correct frame and cycle, not label regression. Label completion cannot verify that the correct level, phase, or beat was used; it can only reproduce the reporting sonographer's choice. This limitation is strongest for `lvot*diam`, `arch*diam`, `sinus*diam`, `ascending*aorta*diameter`, `ivc*diam`, and all wall-thickness fields.

**Family audit.** Most provisional families are clinically coherent as concept groupings but are not valid margin-pooling units. `lv*systolic*geometry` mixes a percentage (`lvef`, `fs`) with linear dimensions — no shared margin. `tr*pulmonary*pressure` mixes m/s, mm Hg, and a linear diameter, and its members have very different reproducibility. `rv*structure*function` duplicates `tricuspid*regurgitant*peak*velocity` and `ivc*diam` across families; TRV is better placed once as a hemodynamic/PH variable with cross-links. `mitral*diastolic` mixes tissue-Doppler annular velocities (cm/s, low absolute magnitude) with inflow velocities (cm/s, several-fold larger) — separate margins required. `anthropometrics` and `hemodynamic*context` are not echo families at all. `atrial_geometry` groups a parasternal AP dimension with two apical lengths measured in different views; acceptable conceptually, not for a shared margin without justification.

## 2. LVEF endpoint ruling

**Preserve LVEF [18][19] The only technical mismatch worth documenting is inequality direction: the guideline construct is ≤40%, while the project endpoint is [18][12][20] That makes it a legitimate prespecified secondary contrast. It must not become primary, and the rationale must be guideline classification, never discrimination performance. Guideline-supported severity bands relevant to interpretation: ≤40% reduced; 41%–49% mildly reduced; ≥50% preserved.[18][19][21] Note the live disagreement: some authors argue for sex-specific normal thresholds (≥55% men, ≥60% women) rather than a single 50% cut; this is a proposal, not guideline, and should not be adopted.[22]

**Continuous LVEF variability.** For 2D biplane Simpson without contrast, interobserver SEM was 0.040 (4.0 EF points) with minimal detectable change 0.111 (11.1 points); intraobserver SEM 0.033 with MDC 0.090; interobserver test-retest SEM 0.047.[23] Non-contrast 3D was substantially better (interobserver SEM 0.027, MDC 0.075).[23] In a dedicated test-retest design across EF 14%–73%, the relative smallest detectable change for EF was 14.2% of the mean value.[24] Beat-to-beat variability of manual EF was 2.7 ± 8.16%.[25] In a guideline-directed normative cohort, 2D LVEF repeatability was among the poorest of all parameters (ICC 0.53).[26]

**Proposed continuous tolerance and tie margin (EXPERT_INFERENCE).** Conditional on `lvef` being in EF percentage points: a clinically meaningful absolute-error tolerance of **±5 EF points** (sensitivity range 4–8), anchored to the interobserver SEM of ~4 points and well inside the 2D MDC of ~11 points.[23] A model-comparison equivalence/tie margin of **1.0 EF point in paired MAE** (sensitivity range 0.5–2.0) — deliberately far below the tolerance, because a tie margin must be smaller than the smallest difference a reader would notice. Both are inferences transferred from reader-variability studies, not validated MCIDs; label them as such.

**Near-threshold uncertainty band.** Supported in principle by the reproducibility data. Given interobserver SEM ~4 EF points and MDC ~11 points for 2D EF, hard classification at 40% is unstable across roughly[23] **35%–45%** (narrower alternative 37%–43% if anchoring to ±1 SEM). Recommended handling: report primary accuracy on all cases, plus a prespecified secondary analysis excluding or separately reporting the band. Do not use the band to redefine the endpoint.

**Report labels as reference truth.** Reported LVEF is frequently visually estimated or rounded to 5% increments; visual EF has wider bias and limits of agreement than manual tracing, and interobserver differences for visual and manual EF are significantly different across centres.[25] Method (biplane, triplane, 3D, contrast, visual) is usually not recoverable from the label, yet materially changes both bias and variance.[23][27] Therefore project error metrics estimate agreement with a single institutional reader under unknown methodology — not accuracy against a physiological truth. Any claim of "expert-level" performance is not supportable from this design.
