# ANESTHESIOLOGY 2026 ePoster scope

## Purpose

The ePoster must remain faithful to the accepted abstract while clearly labeling any post-acceptance revalidation or sensitivity analysis permitted by conference policy.

## Required before final poster export

- Confirm upload deadline and vendor specifications.
- Confirm whether updated analyses are permitted and whether historical results must remain visible.
- Reconcile the selected cohort and embedding-store denominator.
- Preserve the accepted title, presentation number A1122, session, date/time, and presentation format.
- Use the historical LVEF <40% definition.
- If displaying new results, identify them as `post-acceptance revalidation` or `sensitivity analysis`.
- Use `numerically highest` rather than `statistically superior` unless paired intervals support the stronger statement.

## Preferred narrative hierarchy

1. Quantitative echo reports are incomplete and same-report fields can create leakage.
2. Frozen whole-study imaging representations may add information to available structured measurements.
3. Show the denominator funnel and historical accepted LVEF anchor.
4. Show paired incremental-value estimates only after denominator identity is verified.
5. Distinguish strict leakage-minimized prediction from pragmatic same-report completion.
6. Close with limitations, including internal-only validation and observed-label conditioning.

## Preferred figures

- Cohort and artifact-lineage funnel.
- Paired fusion-minus-comparator effect plot with confidence intervals.
- Complete per-task or family-level dot/forest plot on common denominators.
- Compact dependency diagram for LV dimensions/FS, TR velocity/gradient, and duplicate mitral E fields.

Do not use a macro-only bar chart or a `top gains` plot as the primary multitask evidence. Do not claim generative performance, natural-missing-label accuracy, direct measurement automation, clinical deployment, patient-outcome benefit, or perioperative risk prediction.
