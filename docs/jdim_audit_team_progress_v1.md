# JDIM Audit Team-Cumulative Progress V1

- LVOT VTI and TAPSE denominators are fixed at 15 assignments each.
- Progress is cumulative across all qualified reviewers.
- Only effective, current-protocol, finalized and locked Primary reviews count.
- A cross-target Primary study counts once for each target and once physically.
- Secondary reviews never increment target completion.
- Counters are recalculated server-side from the locked roster and review state.
- Contributor output is aggregate-only and never exposes study target membership.
- Owner mutation routes require a separate secret and create immutable action logs.
- Existing records and pre-cutover preset behavior are not migrated or rewritten.
