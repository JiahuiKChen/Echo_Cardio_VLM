## Project-Specific Claims Not Supported By External Literature Alone

These claims should be supported by project Methods, provenance notes, scripts, aggregate outputs, and canonical tables:

- EchoPrime was used as a frozen feature extractor in this project.
- EchoPrime weights were not updated during downstream training.
- Clip-level 512-dimensional embeddings were mean-pooled into study-level all-clips embeddings.
- The trained supervised component was downstream Ridge regression.
- Labels were structured report measurements rather than independent manual remeasurements.
- LVOT VTI and TAPSE metrics, confidence intervals, split sizes, non-image baseline results, and exploratory AUROC values.
- DICOM metadata were insufficient to classify retained clips reliably as Doppler, M-mode, or 2D/cine in this processed pipeline.

## Citation-Safety Concerns Before Submission

- LVOT VTI <18 cm and <20 cm should remain exploratory unless a direct threshold-validation source is added.
- TAPSE <17 mm is clinically grounded, but the project result is a thresholded summary of continuous predictions, not a separately trained classifier.
- Do not cite EchoPrime to imply this study fine-tuned EchoPrime or directly measured LVOT VTI/TAPSE.
- Do not cite direct measurement automation papers to imply this project performed direct automation.
- Do not claim Doppler/M-mode clips were absent; current audit supports only that available metadata could not classify retention reliably.
